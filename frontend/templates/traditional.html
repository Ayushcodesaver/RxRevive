{{ macros.icon('scan') }} How this engine works

  1. Clean and preprocess the resume and job description text (normalisation, symbols, stop words).
  2. Build TF-IDF vectors — unigrams and bigrams weighted by importance across both documents.
  3. Calculate cosine similarity between the two vectors.
  4. Extract skills with a curated taxonomy and word-boundary matching.
  5. Identify matched and missing skills.
  6. Combine similarity (60%) and skill coverage (40%) into the match score.
  7. Generate the analysis report.
