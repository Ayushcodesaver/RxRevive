Two NLP implementations. One problem statement.

The same resume and job description are run through both engines — so every difference in their results is a real, explainable difference in method.

{{ macros.icon('scan') }}

Traditional NLP

TF-IDF vectorization weighs every term by importance; cosine similarity measures the angle between the resume and job description vectors. Fast, transparent and fully explainable — but lexical: it matches words, not meaning.

Transformer NLP

Sentence-BERT encodes both documents into dense semantic embeddings where meaning maps to position. Paraphrases and synonyms score as relevant even when they share no words — contextual understanding beyond keywords.
